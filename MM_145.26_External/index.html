Wildlife use

The area adjacent to the structure is used by pronghorn and mule deer as documented through GPS tracking and recorded roadkill. The structure at MM 145.26 is used by pronghorn to cross the interstate as documented by monitoring with trail cameras. Some of the access to the crossing structure may be limited by the nearby railroad. The area around the crossing structures may see fewer collisions than the adjacent area without access to crossing structures.

Sources: Western Migrations, WYDOT, University of Wyoming.
